01 About

I design, program and validate the electrical systems that products depend on — PLC and HMI logic, embedded firmware, PCB control boards, and the 2D/3D documentation that gets them built — with experience in both the aerospace and manufacturing industries.

Over four years I've worked across a satellite applications centre in the aerospace industry, an industrial automation integrator, and a manufacturing industry business building biomass heating systems. That mix means I'm as comfortable writing C for a microcontroller and probing a board with a spectrum analyzer as I am drafting AutoCAD schematics, running Lean/Six Sigma improvements, or building a Power BI dashboard from test data.

I hold Engineer-in-Training status with Engineers Geoscientists Manitoba and I'm working toward my P.Eng.
